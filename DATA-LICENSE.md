# Data and content licensing

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to the repository's original software and
documentation. It does not replace the terms attached to third-party data.

## Redistributed data

- Natural Earth 1:10m physical vectors, version 5.1.2: public domain. The
  repository redistributes clipped and simplified derivatives.
- GeoNames locality coordinates: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The
  repository records GeoNames identifiers and approximate coordinates.

Exact upstream identities, URLs, hashes, transformations, and attribution are
recorded in `cases/crusader_states/public/ATTRIBUTION.md` and
`cases/crusader_states/public/fixture-manifest.json`.

## Historical publications and reference maps

Historical publications are represented by citations, locators, access checks,
and original paraphrases. Copyrighted book scans, article PDFs, and atlas plates
are not redistributed. Public-domain comparison maps are registered but are not
bundled or traced into reconstruction geometry.

Users who obtain upstream material separately remain responsible for its terms.
